Emote Guide

Twitch Emote Sizes, Rules & Upload Steps

Everything you need to know before you upload your emotes to your channel.

Twitch emote size requirements

Twitch asks for every emote in three square versions so it looks sharp in chat at different zoom levels. Keep the artwork simple — emotes are tiny, so bold shapes and clear faces work best.

ItemRequirement
Sizes112×112, 56×56, 28×28 px
Static formatPNG (transparent background)
Static max size25 KB per file
Animated formatGIF
Animated max size1 MB per file
Animated frames60 or fewer

Tip: start from a 112×112 (or larger) original. Shrinking always loses a little detail, and starting big keeps the smaller sizes crisp.

Emote content guidelines

Emotes are reviewed before they go live, and approved ones can still be removed later if they break the rules. To stay safe:

  • Avoid copyrighted characters, logos and other people's artwork.
  • No violent, sexual, hateful or illegal content, and no nudity (even cartoon).
  • Don't edit or copy existing Twitch emotes unless the rules specifically allow it.
  • Channel emotes need Affiliate or Partner status, and each tier unlocks its own emotes for subscribers.

Always read Twitch's official emote guidelines for the latest rules, since they can change.

How to upload emotes to Twitch

  1. Log in to Twitch and open the Creator Dashboard from the top-right menu.
  2. In the left menu open Viewer Rewards or Preferences (Affiliate/Partner), then go to Emotes.
  3. Pick a tier slot, click upload, and add your 112, 56 and 28 px files.
  4. Name your emote, save it, and wait for the review to finish.

Menu names can differ slightly depending on your account type and Twitch updates.

How to use this emote resizer

  1. Drop your image or GIF into the box above (or click to pick files).
  2. Choose how non-square art is handled: Fit keeps everything with transparent padding, Crop cuts the center square, Stretch forces a square.
  3. Check the preview and the file-size badges, then download each size or grab everything as a ZIP.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about resizing Twitch emotes.

What sizes does Twitch require for emotes?
Twitch emotes are uploaded in three square sizes: 112×112, 56×56 and 28×28 pixels. This tool creates all three at once.
What is the maximum file size for a Twitch emote?
Static PNG emotes should be 25 KB or smaller per file, and animated GIF emotes should be 1 MB or smaller. The resizer shows the size of every file and warns you when one is over the limit.
Can I resize animated GIF emotes?
Yes. Upload a GIF and the tool resizes every frame and keeps the animation and transparency. Twitch animated emotes are limited to 60 frames, and the tool warns you if your GIF has more.
What happens to non-square images?
Emotes must be square, so you can choose Fit (keeps the whole image with transparent padding), Crop (cuts to the center square) or Stretch (forces a square).
Are my emotes uploaded to a server?
No. Everything is processed inside your browser, so your artwork never leaves your device.
Is the Twitch emote resizer free?
Yes. It is 100% free with no login, no watermark and no usage limit.
