What Is a Pie Chart?

A pie chart is a circular graph divided into slices, where each slice shows a category's share of the whole. The full circle is 100%, and each slice's angle is proportional to its value divided by the total. Pie charts work best with a small number of categories (roughly two to seven) when you want to show how a total is split, such as a budget breakdown, survey results, market share or time spent on daily activities. A doughnut chart is a pie chart with a hole in the center, which many people find cleaner for modern reports and dashboards.

When to use a pie chart: parts of a whole that add up to a meaningful total, with few categories and clear differences between slices. When to avoid it: comparing many similar values or showing change over time. A bar chart or line chart is usually clearer for those.

Pie Chart Maker FAQ

How do I make a pie chart online for free?

Type your labels and values in the form above, choose your options, and the pie chart is generated instantly. Then click Download PNG, JPG or SVG.

How do I show percentages on a pie chart?

Set Slice text to Percentage. Each slice shows its share of the total, calculated automatically from your values.

Can I make a 3D pie chart or a doughnut chart?

Yes. Switch on Make 3D for a 3D pie chart, or set Pie hole above 0 (for example 0.5) to make a doughnut chart.

Which file formats can I download?

PNG, JPG and SVG. SVG is a vector format that stays sharp at any size, which makes it ideal for print.

Do the values have to add up to 100?

No. Enter any positive numbers. The chart works out each slice's share of the total.

Is my data saved or uploaded?

No. The chart is drawn entirely in your browser and your data never leaves your device.
