Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about making passport size photos on WikisAI.

Is this passport size photo maker free?
Yes. The WikisAI Passport Size Photo Generator is 100% free with no signup, no watermark and no daily limits. You can create as many passport photos as you need — every day.
What size is a standard passport photo?
Most countries use 3.5 × 4.5 cm (35 × 45 mm) — including India, the UK, Pakistan, Australia and most of Europe. The USA and Philippines use 2 × 2 inch (51 × 51 mm), and Canada uses 5 × 7 cm (50 × 70 mm). Our tool supports all of these plus custom sizes.
Are my photos uploaded to a server?
No. All processing happens locally inside your browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your photo never leaves your device, which makes the tool completely private and secure.
Can I change the background colour of my passport photo?
Yes. Turn on the automatic background removal option and choose white, off-white, light blue, blue, red, grey, or any custom colour using the built-in colour picker.
Can I print multiple passport photos on one sheet?
Yes. The download step includes a print-sheet builder for A4, 4 × 6 inch and Letter paper. It automatically packs as many copies as fit and can add cutting lines for easy trimming.
Does it work on mobile phones?
Yes. The tool is fully responsive and works on Android phones, iPhones, tablets, laptops and desktop computers in any modern browser such as Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox or Opera.
Will my photo quality be reduced?
No. The output uses the original image data with a 300 or 600 DPI print resolution. Your downloaded passport photo will be sharp and print-ready.
