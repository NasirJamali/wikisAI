Pakistan Mobile Number Prefixes & Networks

A prefix only tells you where a number started. Under Mobile Number Portability (MNP) a subscriber can switch operators and keep the same number, so a 0300 number may now run on another network. Use PTA's number-network SMS service to confirm the current one.

Official & Legal

How to Check SIM Owner Details the Right Way

These PTA channels show SIM information tied to your own identity. Codes can change, so confirm them on pta.gov.pk before use.

Check the SIMs on your own CNIC

  1. Open cnic.sims.pk or SMS your 13-digit CNIC (no dashes) to 668.
  2. Read the count of SIMs registered per operator.
  3. Screenshot it with the date and compare month to month.
  4. If a SIM isn't yours, report it to the operator and PTA, then disown it at a franchise with your original CNIC.

Verify a SIM you physically hold

  1. Insert the SIM in your phone.
  2. Use PTA's verification SMS service (commonly published as 667).
  3. For any number's current network, use the number-network SMS service (commonly 76367).
  4. Message PTA's official RAABTA assistant on WhatsApp for guided help.

⚠️ Why this page never shows a stranger's name, CNIC or address

Subscriber records are held in PTA's closed registry. No public website has lawful access to it. Pages that return a person's name, CNIC or location from a phone number are either inventing the result or reselling leaked data — and they collect the numbers you type in. Accessing or using someone else's identity information without authorisation can be an offence under the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016.

If you are being harassed, threatened or scammed by an unknown number, keep screenshots and report it to the NCCIA cyber helpline (1799) and to PTA. Those bodies can lawfully request subscriber data; a lookup website cannot.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about SIM number details in Pakistan.

Can I find the owner name of a mobile number in Pakistan?
Not through any public tool. Subscriber records are held in PTA's closed registry and are released only to the CNIC holder for their own CNIC, or to authorised agencies through legal process. Sites that claim to show a stranger's name, CNIC or address are fabricating results or using leaked data.
How do I know which network a number belongs to?
The first four digits show the network the number was originally issued on, for example 0300 to 0309 is Jazz and 0330 to 0338 is Ufone. Because of Mobile Number Portability, the current network can differ, so confirm with PTA's number-network SMS service.
How can I check all SIMs registered on my own CNIC?
Use PTA's official channels: SMS your 13-digit CNIC to 668, or use the cnic.sims.pk portal. Both show how many SIMs are registered on your CNIC. Confirm current instructions on pta.gov.pk.
Why does the tool say "Data not found" for name, CNIC and location?
Because that information is private and not publicly available. The tool only shows what can be derived lawfully from the number itself: format, prefix and original network.
Is it legal to look up someone else's SIM details?
Accessing or using another person's identity information without authorisation can be an offence under the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016. If you are being harassed, report it to the NCCIA (helpline 1799) instead of trying to identify the person yourself.
Does ONIC still have its own prefix?
The 0339 range is listed as ONIC-associated, but allocations and brand arrangements change. Treat it as a hint and verify with the operator or PTA.
