Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our background remover.

Is this background remover really free?
Yes. This tool is 100% free with no signup, no watermark and no limits. You can use it as many times as you like.
Are my photos uploaded to a server?
No. Everything is processed inside your browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your image never leaves your device.
What type of photo works best?
Photos with a plain, evenly-lit background work best. Try a lower tolerance for busy backgrounds and a higher tolerance for very plain ones.
