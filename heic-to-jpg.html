Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about HEIC conversion.

What is HEIC?
HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the format iPhones use by default. It offers better compression than JPG, but many apps and websites don't support it — that's why converting to JPG is useful.
Are my photos uploaded?
No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser using a JavaScript HEIC decoder. Your photos never leave your device.
Does conversion reduce quality?
At 92% quality, the difference is virtually invisible. You can increase the slider for even higher quality (larger files) or lower it for smaller files.
