Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the Image to PDF converter.

How many images can I combine?
There's no hard limit, but we recommend keeping it under 50 images for smooth browser performance.
Are my images uploaded?
No. Everything is processed inside your browser. Your files never leave your device.
What formats are supported?
JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF (first frame), and BMP are supported.
Can I reorder the pages?
Yes. Images are added to the PDF in the order you select them. To reorder, remove and re-add them in the order you want.
