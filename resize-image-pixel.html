Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about resizing images by pixel.

What does "lock aspect ratio" do?
It keeps the original width-to-height ratio so your image never looks stretched. When you change the width, the height updates automatically (and vice versa).
What's the maximum size I can resize to?
Up to 10,000 × 10,000 pixels. Going much larger than the original may reduce sharpness, so use our Upscale tool for better results.
Are my images uploaded?
No. Everything is processed inside your browser using HTML5 Canvas.

Need a specific KB size?
