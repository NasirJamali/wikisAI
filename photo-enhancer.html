Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about enhancing photos with WikisAI.

Is this photo enhancer free?
Yes. The WikisAI Photo Enhancer is 100% free with no signup, no watermark and no daily limits. You can enhance as many photos as you like, every day.
Are my photos uploaded to a server?
No. All enhancement happens locally inside your browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your photo never leaves your device, which makes this tool completely private and secure.
Can I upscale an image to 4K?
Yes. Choose the 2× or 4× upscale option in the panel. A 1080p photo becomes ~4K when using 4× upscale. For best results, keep the Sharpness slider around 30–50%.
What type of photos benefit most?
Photos with soft focus, mild blur, high-ISO noise, or low resolution see the biggest improvement. Old scanned photos and heavily-compressed images also benefit greatly.
Does enhancement change my photo?
No. The tool only improves clarity, color, sharpness and noise. It preserves the original composition, faces and structure exactly — you can verify with the before/after slider.
Does it work on mobile phones?
Yes. The tool is fully responsive and works on Android phones, iPhones, tablets, laptops and desktop computers in any modern browser such as Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox.
What image formats are supported?
You can upload JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF and most other image formats. Download is available as JPG or PNG.
