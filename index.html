Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about WikisAI and our free tools.

Is WikisAI really 100% free?
Yes. Every tool on WikisAI is completely free to use with no hidden charges, no watermarks and no credit card required. You can use any tool as many times as you like, every day.
Do I need to create an account or sign up?
No. WikisAI requires no registration, no email address and no login. Just open a tool, upload your file, and download the result.
Are my images and files safe?
Yes. Most WikisAI tools process your files entirely inside your browser, so your images never leave your device. Any file that is uploaded to our servers is automatically and permanently deleted within 30 minutes.
What is the best tool to make a passport size photo?
Use the WikisAI Passport Photo Maker. It supports 3.5 × 4.5 cm, 2 × 2 inch, 5 × 7 cm and fully custom sizes, with cropping, background removal, brightness and contrast controls, plus a print-ready A4 sheet download containing multiple copies.
Can I use WikisAI tools on my phone?
Absolutely. WikisAI is fully responsive and works on Android phones, iPhones, tablets, laptops and desktop computers in any modern browser such as Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox or Opera.
Is there a file size limit?
Most tools accept images up to 25 MB per file. The Bulk Image Resizer and our PDF tools support larger files. There is no daily usage limit and no cap on the number of files you can process.
Will my compressed image lose quality?
Our compression tools use smart algorithms that reduce file size while preserving as much visual quality as possible. For most form uploads the difference is completely invisible to the human eye.
